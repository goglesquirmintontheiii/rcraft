## RustCraft 0.2.0 — Phase 2: survival

Multiple worlds, crafting and smelting, hunger and XP, sounds, mobs, weather,
flowing water, and Java Edition world import/export. Issue #1 is worked
through item by item (see the issue for notes on each).

**Download**
- Windows: `rustcraft-0.2.0-windows-x86_64.exe` (portable, just run it)
- Linux: `rustcraft-0.2.0-linux-x86_64.AppImage` (`chmod +x`, then run)

0.1.0 installs with automatic updates on will offer this version from
Options → Updates.

### New
- **Worlds**: as many worlds as you like, with rename, delete, backup and a
  create screen for seed, game mode, difficulty and game rules.
- **Java Edition import/export**: import a 1.18–1.21.1 world from
  `.minecraft/saves` (blocks, biomes, your position, inventory, weather, game
  rules), or export a RustCraft world to a folder Java 1.21.1 opens.
- **Survival**: crafting (2×2 and crafting table) and smelting (furnace,
  blast furnace, smoker) with every vanilla recipe; tools with vanilla mining
  speeds, correct-tool drops and durability; hunger, saturation, eating,
  regeneration; experience orbs and levels.
- **Blocks that do things**: chests and barrels, beds (sleep through the
  night), doors, trapdoors, fence gates, ladders and vines, farming (hoe,
  seeds, crops, bone meal), saplings, leaf decay, falling sand and gravel,
  buckets, flint and steel, cake.
- **Water and lava flow**, and make obsidian and cobblestone when they meet.
- **Mobs**: cows, pigs, sheep and chickens with vanilla-style wandering,
  panicking, following food, breeding and babies; shear and dye sheep, milk
  cows, chickens lay eggs. They spawn in suitable biomes as you explore.
- **Dropped items** on the ground, merged and capped for performance;
  press Q to drop (Ctrl+Q for the stack).
- **Day and night, rain, snow, thunderstorms and lightning**; `/time`,
  `/weather`.
- **Sound** from your Minecraft install: blocks, footsteps, mobs, UI, rain
  and music, with a Music & Sounds volume screen.
- **Your hand and held item** in first person, the player model in the
  inventory, block-breaking particles.
- **Commands**: Tab autocompletion with argument hints, selectors (`@s`,
  `@p`, `@a`, `@r`, `@e[type=…,limit=…]`) for `/tp`, plus `/summon`,
  `/weather`, `/difficulty`, `/gamerule`, `/xp`, `/setworldspawn`, `/say`.
- **Creative inventory** with vanilla tabs; search lives in its own tab.
- Render distance up to **128 chunks**.
- Options → Performance → **Open Data Folder**.

### Fixed (issue #1)
- Item icons fit their slots; glass and other crafted blocks left the
  Natural tab.
- Leaves and flowers no longer flicker or look too see-through.
- Smoother lighting curve; title and menu screens have a background even
  without the panorama.
- The memory limit is now enforced against actual usage: the view distance
  steps down while the game is over the limit.
- Smooth FOV change when sprinting; inventory scrolling no longer races on
  touchpads.

### Textures and sounds
RustCraft never ships Mojang assets. With Minecraft Java 1.21.1 installed
(run it once from the launcher), its textures, fonts, sounds and music are
used automatically; otherwise RustCraft uses its own procedural textures and
is silent.

### Known limitations (planned for 0.3)
Redstone, minecarts, hostile mobs and structures; the remaining
"special" blocks (signs, enchanting, brewing, anvils…) and passive mobs
(horses, wolves, villagers…); key rebinding; the recipe book; Nether and End
are generated but not reachable yet.
